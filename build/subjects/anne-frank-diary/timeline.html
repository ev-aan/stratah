People and bodies involved

  • Anne Frank: Wrote the diary (born 12 June 1929; died in Bergen-Belsen in early 1945). The question concerns the document, not her.
  • Otto Frank: Her father, the only one of the eight in hiding to survive; compiled the 1947 edition; died 19 August 1980; bequeathed the manuscripts to the Dutch state.
  • Miep Gies and Bep Voskuijl: Helpers who found Anne's papers after the arrest; Miep Gies kept them and gave them to Otto Frank.
  • Gerrit Bolkestein: Dutch education minister in exile; appealed on Radio Oranje on 28 March 1944 for diaries and letters to be kept.
  • Mirjam Pressler: Compiled the 1991 reader's edition (version D) for the Anne Frank Fonds.
  • Gerrold van der Stroom and David Barnouw: Editors of the 1986 Critical Edition at the Dutch state war documentation institute; van der Stroom is the author of a source read here.
  • Robert Faurisson: Author of a 1978 report for a Hamburg court that denied the diary's authenticity; his report was offered in the Frankfurt appeal, and he was a defendant in the Amsterdam case. His claims are recorded in transmission.yaml as reception only.
