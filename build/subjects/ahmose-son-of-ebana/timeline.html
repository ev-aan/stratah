People and bodies involved

  • R. Lepsius: Led the Prussian expedition of 1842 to 1845 whose drawn copy of the tomb text is Denkmaeler Abth. III Bl. 12 (title pages, per the dig's first pass).
  • J. H. Breasted: Translated the text in Ancient Records of Egypt II (1906) and says he collated the Berlin squeeze no. 172.
  • K. Sethe: Edited the hieroglyphic text (Urk. IV 1-11, 1906) and published a German translation (1914).
  • M. Lichtheim: Translated the text in Ancient Egyptian Literature II (the printing Davies cites is 1976; the upload read is of a later edition).
  • W. V. Davies: Reported the British Museum survey of the tomb in 2009 (OLA 191), with corrected readings of some columns.
