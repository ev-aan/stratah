People and bodies involved

  • Edward M. Chen: US District Judge (N.D. Cal.) who issued the 24 Sep 2024 findings in Food & Water Watch v. EPA; vacated on appeal 21 May 2026.
  • Robert F. Kennedy Jr.: US Secretary of Health and Human Services from 2025; posted on 2 Nov 2024 that fluoride is 'an industrial waste' associated with IQ loss; said in Apr 2025 he would tell the CDC to stop recommending fluoridation (AP).
  • Lee Zeldin: EPA Administrator; announced the EPA's review of fluoride on 7 Apr 2025.
  • Philippe Grandjean: Senior author of Choi et al. 2012 (the 'Harvard study') and of later benchmark-dose analyses.
  • Christine Till: Senior author of the Canadian MIREC fluoride studies (2019, 2020).
