People and bodies involved

  • W. M. Flinders Petrie: Excavator of the temple of Merneptah at Thebes in 1896; author of Six Temples at Thebes (1897).
  • Wilhelm Spiegelberg: Published the text and a commentary in Zeitschrift fuer aegyptische Sprache 34 (1896) and the translation in Petrie 1897.
  • James Henry Breasted: Translated the stele and the Karnak and Athribis texts in Ancient Records of Egypt, vol. 3 (1906).
  • Pierre Lacau: Wrote the 1909 Cairo Museum catalogue entry (Catalogue general 34025).
  • Johannes Duemichen: Published the Karnak fragment of a duplicate in Historische Inschriften I (1867).
  • Peter van der Veen, Christoffer Theis, Manfred Goerg: Authors of the 2010 article proposing an earlier writing of the name on a Berlin relief.
  • Ingrid Hjelm: Author of the 2016 chapter that says scholars have disagreed about how the hieroglyphs of the name should be read.
