History's earliest testimony of an entity named "Israel" (Ysr'el) is found on the Merneptah Stele depicting the Egyptian Pharaoh Merneptah's campaign to Hatti-land around 1209 BCE (Pritchard 1969: 376-78).
Scholars have disagreed about what the term signifies and how the hieroglyphs should be read as there are several possibilities. From the more remote Sharon and Jizreel as well as Biblical Jeshurun to the closer reading 'Asher [...] (Hjelm and Thompson 2002: 13-14).
The Merneptah stele's designation of Hurru's husband as "Israel" with the determinative of a people rather than land is exceptional in Egyptian epigraphy.
[the closing lines] "Israel is laid waste, its seed is no more" [...] relegate this to conventional and hyperbolic phraseology (e.g. Kitchen 2003: 220).
