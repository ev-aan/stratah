People and bodies involved

  • Benny Gantz: Israeli Defense Minister; signed the 2021 designations of Samidoun and the six NGOs (NBCTF).
  • Ehud Barak: Israeli Defense Minister; reported as signing the 2008 order against 36 Union of Good funds, including IHH (Ynet; ITIC).
  • Benjamin Netanyahu: Israeli Prime Minister; defended allowing Qatari cash into Gaza in Nov 2018 (AFP report). A profile is planned as a separate dig.
  • Philippe Lazzarini: UNRWA Commissioner-General; announced the dismissals of staff named by Israel in Jan 2024 (UN statement).
  • Catherine Colonna: Led the independent review of UNRWA's neutrality, reported 20 Apr 2024.
  • David Cameron; David Lammy: UK Foreign Secretaries who announced settler sanctions (Feb and May 2024; Oct 2024) and, for Lammy, the restoration of UNRWA funding (Jul 2024).
  • Ahmed Hussen: Canadian minister who paused (26 Jan 2024) and resumed (8 Mar 2024) UNRWA funding.
