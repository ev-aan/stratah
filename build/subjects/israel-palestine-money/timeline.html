As of 2026-10-16. Times are UTC; every event here is known to the day or month only, so dots are drawn at noon on the day. "Single" means one source was read for the event; "reported" means two or more agree. Each event's detail states what the cited document records. Dots show dates only and say nothing about cause (rule P5). Panel C enlarges October 2023 to October 2026. Lanes list every channel read under one written rule for the channel (see the claims named in the dig file); the FEC lane lists committees by receipts band and in alphabetical order, not by side. The Israel entries in the FARA lane carry onward-payment and budget detail that the entries for the other countries do not (exhibits and onward payments were read for Israel-labelled registrants; for the other labels only principal-level Item 14(a) lines were read).
