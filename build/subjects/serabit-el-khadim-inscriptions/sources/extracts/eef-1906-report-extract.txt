Sinai. Petrie's Researches in Sinai is an account of his work for the Egypt Exploration Fund in 1904-5 and Mr. Currelly's for the Egyptian Research Account in the same year. [...] Besides the account of the journey, the book contains a full description of the mines and inscribed tablets of Maghareh, and the bethels, mines and Hathor-temple of Serabit, where a few examples of a non-Egyptian script were found contemporary with the XVIIIth Dynasty.
