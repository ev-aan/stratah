People and bodies involved

  • Claude F.-A. Schaeffer: Directed the Ras Shamra excavations from 1929; the 18th to 20th campaigns (1954-56) are listed as directed by him (Saade 2011, pp. 26-27). Interpreted the Court V find as an oven for baking tablets (as reported by Millard, Lombard, Saade).
  • Michael C. Astour: Wrote in 1965 that the lot was found in an oven for baking tablets and that two letters may be unbaked originals.
  • Yves Calvet: Resumed the excavation of the Court V area in 1979 (Lombard n. 10); wrote in Syria 67 (1990) that the interpretation of the oven as a tablet oven does not hold (as quoted by Millard and Lombard; not read).
  • Alan Millard, Pierre Lombard: Authors of papers in Ras Shamra-Ougarit XI (1995) that report the revision.
  • Eric H. Cline: Author of 1177 B.C. (2014; revised 2021), where the ship letter is described as first reported found in a kiln.
