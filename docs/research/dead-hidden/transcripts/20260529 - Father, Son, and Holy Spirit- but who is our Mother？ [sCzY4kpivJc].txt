Wherefore comfort one another with these words. That is the marriage of the bride to Christ. What follows is Armageddon, the marriage supper of the Lamb. We are the children of God by the gospel of Jesus Christ. He is our everlasting Father in heaven. Holy Jerusalem above is our mother, and we are the citizens thereof. God bless you and keep you.
